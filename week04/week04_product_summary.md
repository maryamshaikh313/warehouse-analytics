## Findings
- 10 of the 30 products generate about 82% of sales.
- Engine Block EB-600 (4,856,985,000) and Transmission Assembly TA-800 (3,387,929,000) together generate about 36% of sales.
- Units sold per product range from 39,486 to 42,702, a difference of about 8%.
- Fast, Medium and Slow labels are relative groups of 10 products each. They do not indicate slow-moving or dead stock.
- Because unit volumes are similar, differences in sales come mainly from price. Engine Block EB-600 is relatively "Slow" by units but is class A by value.
- Product-level sales add up to 22,958,987,430, matching the total from sales order headers.

## Recommendations
- Monitor stock availability of class A products most closely, especially P015 and P014.
- Treat the dependence on these two products as a risk to review.
- Use value (ABC) rather than the movement labels to set priorities.
