## Insights
- The top 5 products account for about 59% of sales, and P015 alone about 21%. This assumes line totals match order header totals, which is not yet checked.
- Sales were highest in 2020 and lowest in 2024 (about 3.8% below 2023 and 6.1% below 2020).
- 15.1% of received purchase orders arrived after the expected date.
- The causes of cancellations, late deliveries and the 2024 decline need further analysis.
